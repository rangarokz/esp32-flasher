ESP32 Firmware Flasher

Connect your ESP32 board via USB to flash either the Accelerator or Brake controller firmware directly from your browser.

🚄

Train Accelerator Controller

FIRMWARE V1.0.0 • READY

Flashes the throttle & reverser controller with calibrated notch detents, ADC potentiometer sampling, and direction switches.

Target Chip ESP32 / ESP32-S3 (2MB+ Flash)
Potentiometer Pin GPIO 34 (ADC1 Channel 6)
Reverser Switches GPIO 4 (Fwd), GPIO 16 (Rev)
Notch Calibration Onboard NVS Flash Storage
⚠️ Web Serial is not supported on this browser. Please open in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on Desktop.
🛑

Train Brake Controller

FIRMWARE V1.0.0 • READY

Flashes the locomotive brake lever controller with calibrated pneumatic detents (Release, Run, Service notches, Emergency).

Target Chip ESP32 / ESP32-S3 (2MB+ Flash)
Brake Lever ADC GPIO 32 (ADC1 Channel 4)
Calibration Button GPIO 0 (BOOT Button)
Status Indicator GPIO 2 (Onboard LED)
⚠️ Web Serial is not supported on this browser. Please open in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on Desktop.

💡 Quick Flashing Tips

  • Use a Data-capable Micro-USB / USB-C cable (not a charge-only cable).
  • If the connection gets stuck on "Connecting...", press and hold the BOOT button on the ESP32 while clicking Connect, then release it when flashing begins.
  • No installation or drivers needed! Works directly in Chrome and Edge.
